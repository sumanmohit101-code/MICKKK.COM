Getting Started & Logging Trades

A trading journal is not just an accounting record — it is your personal feedback mechanism to measure risk discipline, system expectancy, and emotional mistakes.

Manual Entry

Click Log Trade, enter the symbol, buy price, quantity, stop-loss, and target. If the trade is still open, leave the Exit Price field empty.

Broker Import

Click Import Broker Report to upload your official Excel/CSV execution trade ledger from Zerodha or Groww. Trades are matched automatically.

Portfolio Capital

Click Portfolio Capital in the top bar anytime to update your total account size. All portfolio risk percentages dynamically recalculate.

02

Positions Dashboard & The "Target Sell Qty" Metric

The Positions Dashboard gives you real-time visibility into open market risk and float. Click Refresh LTP at any time during market hours to sync real-time NSE market prices.

Risk-Free Positions

A trade is officially classified as Risk-Free when your active Stop-Loss or Trailing Stop-Loss has been moved to or above your breakeven entry price. This means you have zero downside financial risk left on the trade.

Target Sell Qty (Automated Partial Profit Lock)

What it is: Instead of guessing how many shares to sell when a stock is surging, the terminal looks at your historical Average Profit per Trade (Avg P&L) benchmark.

How it works: At the current live LTP, it calculates the exact number of shares needed to lock in that target profit. You can sell that quantity, bank a standard winning trade, and let the remaining balance run completely risk-free!

03

Core Performance Metrics Explained

Win Rate (%)

Percentage of closed trades that resulted in positive net profit: Wins ÷ (Wins + Losses) × 100.

Profit Factor

Total gross winning rupees divided by total gross losing rupees. A factor above 1.50 indicates a solidly profitable trading edge.

R-Multiple & R:R

1R represents your initial planned stop-loss risk. If you risk ₹1,000 to make ₹3,000, that trade realized a +3.0R return.

Avg P&L / Trade

Your statistical expectancy per trade. It proves whether your edge generates reliable positive expected value over a large sample of executions.

Avg Win vs Avg Loss

Compares the average magnitude of your winners against your losers. A great trader keeps average loss small and lets winners run.

Portfolio Open Risk

The total amount of your overall account capital that would be lost if every single open position hit its Stop-Loss simultaneously.

04

Advanced Trade Fields (TSL, Pyramiding & Scale-Outs)

Inside the Trade Log modal, open the + Advanced Fields section to log institutional position management tactics:

1. Trailing Stop-Loss (Trailing SL ₹)

As a stock moves in your favor, you raise your stop-loss price. Entering a Trailing SL overrides the initial SL and updates your Open Risk and Risk-Free badges automatically.

2. Pyramiding Groups & Legs

When adding to a winning position as it breaks out of subsequent bases, group the entries together (e.g. Group: RELIANCE-01, Leg: 2). This enables you to track base trades and add-ons individually or as an overall campaign.

3. Partial Exits (Scale-Out Batches)

Professional swing traders rarely sell 100% of a position all at once. Use + Add Exit to record partial sales (e.g., sell 50 shares at ₹520, then remaining 50 at ₹560). The journal computes blended exit prices and exact brokerage accounting automatically.

05

MFE, MAE & Institutional Execution Diagnostics

Excursion analysis was developed by hedge funds to diagnose whether trade problems stem from poor entries or premature exits.

MFE (Maximum Favorable Excursion)

What it measures: The highest unrealized profit percentage the stock reached between the day you bought it and the day you sold it.

Actionable Insight: If your average MFE is +12% but your average realized gain is only +3%, you are exiting far too early out of fear and leaving massive profits on the table.

MAE (Maximum Adverse Excursion)

What it measures: The deepest unrealized drawdown percentage the trade dipped into against your entry price before exiting.

Actionable Insight: If your winning trades consistently suffer from a -6% MAE before turning around, your entry timing is premature (you are buying too early in pullbacks instead of waiting for confirmation).

Exit Efficiency (MFE+)

Actual Realized Return ÷ MFE %. If a stock moved up +10% and you captured +8%, your exit efficiency is an outstanding 80%.

Heat Analysis (MAE ÷ Planned Risk)

Measures how close the trade came to triggering your Stop-Loss. If trades frequently touch 95% heat, your stop-losses are positioned too close to market noise.

06

Position Sizing Calculator & R-Target Ladders

Never guess position size. Navigate to the Risk & Sizing Tools tab before placing any live order:

Position Sizing Formula

Shares Quantity = (Portfolio Capital × Risk Tolerance %) ÷ (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)

This mathematical formula guarantees that regardless of stock volatility, hitting your Stop-Loss will never exceed your predefined risk tolerance (e.g., 1.5% or 2.0% of total capital).

R-Multiple Target Ladders

Generates predetermined profit checkpoints: +1R, +2R, +3R, +5R based on the distance between Entry and Stop-Loss.

As the live stock price crosses each level, the terminal flags it as Triggered, reminding you to scale out partial quantity or trail your stop-loss.
